What is a Triathlon?

Not the swim-bike-run kind — ours is a battle of minds. Triathlon 8.0 is LGS OPF's flagship interdisciplinary competition, bringing together three very different arenas — Arts, Business, and Science — under one roof for four days of debate, pitching, problem-solving, and performance.

Every participant picks their track and goes head-to-head with the sharpest minds across schools, proving that talent doesn't live in just one subject. Whether you paint, pitch, or prove theorems — there's a floor here for you.

Categories

Three Tracks. One Trophy.

Arts

Performance, design, and storytelling floors — where creative instinct is the currency and originality wins rounds.

Business

Pitches, case studies, and negotiation rounds that reward sharp strategy and the nerve to defend it under pressure.

Science

Experiments, theorems, and rapid-fire problem sets — built for those who'd rather prove it than say it.
