A Note From The Team

At Lahore Grammar School Senior Girls OPF, we believe education extends beyond the classroom. Triathlon 8.0 is a reflection of that vision — bringing together talented students from across Lahore as a platform for creativity, innovation, and collaboration.

Through its three flagship divisions — Art Nouveau, BizzFest, and Enova — Triathlon celebrates excellence across the arts, business, science, and beyond, offering over 25 diverse categories for students to showcase their talents and ideas. We cordially invite your institution to be part of Triathlon 8.0, taking place from 17th–20th September 2026 at LGS Senior Girls OPF.

Warm regards,
Team Triathlon 8.0

  • Street Sm-art — Live graffiti-inspired art on a bold, life-sized canvas.
  • Salvage — 3D and mixed-media sculpture built from unconventional materials.
  • Phantasmagoria — Turn a surreal random prompt into an extraordinary still-life piece.
  • Picasso's Detective — Decipher clues and sketch your discoveries on the spot.
